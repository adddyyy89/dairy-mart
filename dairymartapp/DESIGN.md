# Design System Document
 
This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our design system, reflecting the current state of our design tokens.
 
## 🎨 Color Palette
 
Our color palette is carefully selected to ensure consistency and reinforce brand identity across all applications.
 
### Color Mode
The system is currently configured for a **light** color scheme, optimizing for readability and visual comfort in bright environments.
 
### Primary Colors
- **Primary Color**: `#1A73E8` - This is our brand's most distinctive chromatic color, ideal for key interactive elements like buttons and calls-to-action.
- **Neutral Color**: `#F8F9FA` - A foundational neutral base used for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic elements, ensuring a clean and accessible user interface.
 
## ✒️ Typography
 
Typography plays a crucial role in conveying information and establishing brand voice.
 
### Font Families
- **Headline Font**: `Inter` - Used for all prominent headings and titles, ensuring strong visual hierarchy.
- **Body Font**: `Inter` - Applied to all main text content, prioritizing legibility and a comfortable reading experience.
- **Label Font**: `Inter` - Employed for labels, captions, and smaller text elements, maintaining consistency across the UI.
 
## 📐 Spacing and Layout
 
Effective spacing enhances readability and guides the user's eye through the interface.
 
### Spacing Scale
The spacing scale is set to `2` (Normal), providing a balanced amount of whitespace around and within UI elements for optimal clarity and organization.
 
## ✨ Shape and Form
 
The treatment of corners and edges contributes significantly to the overall feel of our interface.
 
### Roundedness
UI elements feature a `2` (Moderate) level of roundedness, striking a balance between modern aesthetics and approachability.
